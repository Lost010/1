🔗 Link Hasher

HMAC one-way hash for URLs — runs entirely in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.

✓ Copied!
How it works: HMAC(algorithm, key=seed, message=URL). The seed acts as a private key — the same URL + seed always produces the same hash, but you cannot reverse it back to the original URL. Use the Verify tab to check whether a URL + seed matches a known hash.
What this does: Re-computes the HMAC of the URL + seed and checks if it equals the hash you provide. Accepts both hex and base64url formats. A match means the URL and seed are authentic — a mismatch means something changed or the seed is wrong.
All computation happens in your browser via the Web Crypto API. No data leaves your device.
